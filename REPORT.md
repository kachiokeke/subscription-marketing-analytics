# Recommendation for the Marketing Team

## Headline

Test a controlled budget increase for the strongest creator-led campaign, protect the highest-revenue campaign as the main scale driver, and review the weakest-performing creator campaign before allocating additional spend.

## What We Found

* **One creator-led campaign showed the strongest observed efficiency.** It delivered the best overall return on ad spend and the lowest cost per trial during the available period.
* **The main social campaign generated the most total revenue.** It combined scale with strong efficiency, although its acquisition cost was higher than the most efficient campaign.
* **Another creator-led campaign showed the weakest observed performance.** It had the lowest return, the weakest sale rate, and the highest cost per sale among the mapped campaigns.
* **Onboarding experience showed the clearest relationship with trial and sale outcomes.** However, the available predictors did not reliably distinguish which individual users would convert.

## What We Recommend

* Increase spend on the strongest creator-led campaign gradually and monitor whether ROAS, cost per trial, and sale rate remain healthy as budget increases.
* Maintain the highest-revenue campaign as the main scale channel, while testing ways to reduce its acquisition cost.
* Review the weakest campaign's targeting, creative, and placement before allocating additional budget. Reduce or reallocate spend if performance does not improve.
* Use onboarding-model findings to prioritise controlled experiments rather than individual targeting.
* Do not deploy the current prediction models as automated targeting systems without stronger predictive signal.

## Caveats

* The analysis reflects the available observation period and may not represent long-term campaign performance.
* The results show associations rather than causation. Controlled experiments are required before concluding that a campaign or onboarding experience caused better outcomes.
* One unmapped campaign is retained in user-level analysis but excluded from spend-based efficiency rankings because no corresponding campaign metadata or spend is available.
* The predictive models use a limited set of onboarding-time features and showed weak individual-level discrimination.
